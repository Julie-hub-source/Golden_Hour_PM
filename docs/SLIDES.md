## Exporting the deck

The deck is built with the Slides type. To share it as a file:
1. Open the deck link.
2. Use the **Download** or **Export** option on the page, and choose **PDF** (for uploading to most forms) or **PPTX** (for editing in PowerPoint).

Screenshots in the deck were taken from the live app on the demo project, so they show sample data.
